Data Sources & Methodology

Transparency is important to us. Below are the public datasets, models, and references used by the SmartBuild 3D rule engines to provide estimates and recommendations.

Important Disclaimer: All data is generalized for educational and planning purposes. Construction costs, local regulations, and geographic risks change frequently. Do not use this tool as a substitute for certified professional assessment.

How We Use Data

Floor Plan Generation

The layout generator uses a rule-based algorithm that optimizes for NBC guidelines, ensuring minimum room sizes, adequate ventilation pathways, and logical flow (e.g., placing the kitchen near the dining area). Vastu compliance algorithms are applied as secondary heuristics.

Cost Estimation

Cost is calculated using a base rate multiplier method. The total built-up area is multiplied by a regional base rate, which is then adjusted based on the selected material quality, number of floors, and interior finish styles. A standard 5% contingency is added to account for unforeseen expenses.

Risk Analysis

Location data is mapped to our regional hazard database. The engine flags any intersections with known high-risk zones (e.g., Seismic Zone IV/V or historical flood plains) and generates automated architectural recommendations like raised plinths or reinforced foundations.
