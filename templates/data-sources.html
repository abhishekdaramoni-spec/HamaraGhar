Data Provenance & Calculation Methodology

Transparency is a core engineering principle at HamaraGhar. Below are the statutory building codes, government schedule of rates, and meteorological datasets powering our automated planning algorithms.

Statutory Disclaimer: All calculations, models, and estimations are planning heuristics designed for residential pre-construction feasibility. Final engineering drawings, structural stability certificates, and local municipal sanctions must be executed by licensed professionals.

Algorithmic Processing Pipelines

1. 2D Blueprint Constraint Solver

When plot dimensions and BHK counts are input, the 2D plan generator first establishes statutory setbacks (front 5ft, sides 3ft, rear 4ft) per municipal bye-laws. It then assigns rooms based on Vastu orientation heuristics (Master Bedroom in SW, Kitchen in SE, Pooja in NE) while enforcing NBC room-size minimums and wall-perimeter circulation.

2. Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Multiplier

Total built-up footprint is multiplied by CPWD base rates across 4 material quality tiers (Basic: ₹1,200/sqft, Standard: ₹1,800/sqft, Premium: ₹2,800/sqft, Luxury: ₹4,500/sqft). Civil structure, MEP, openings, and finishes are disaggregated into sub-trade percentages with an 8% inflation and contingency buffer.

3. Multi-Hazard Resilience Index

Our risk engine queries NDMA and IMD regional hazard mappings across 40+ major Indian cities. It cross-references the city's seismic zone (IS 1893) and rainfall flood index to generate a 0–100 Resilience Score, providing specific structural mandates (e.g. 135° ductile ties, plinth elevation) to mitigate vulnerability.

4. Interactive 3D Elevation Studio

3D house geometry is computed client-side using CSS 3D matrix transformations and HTML5 Canvas rendering. Real-time updates immediately cascade changes in number of floors, wall materials, roof profiles, and color swatches directly into structural volume and budget metrics.
